## 3. The problem (use this in discovery)

Service businesses lose revenue in three predictable ways. Lead with these before features.

### Problem 1 — Leads die before anyone replies

Enquiries land on WhatsApp, Facebook, personal phones, and walk-ins. Without one system, response times stretch to hours. Research consistently shows leads are **9× more likely to qualify** when the first response is within five minutes.

**Discovery questions**
- Where do your enquiries come from today?
- How long does it usually take for someone to reply?
- Who owns a lead when it arrives after hours or on a weekend?

### Problem 2 — Pipeline walks out with the salesperson

Contacts, chat history, and relationships sit on personal devices. When a rep leaves, the business starts again from zero.

**Discovery questions**
- If your top salesperson left tomorrow, would you still have every open deal and conversation?
- How do you hand over a lead between reps today?

### Problem 3 — Completed work stays invisible

Strong projects live in camera rolls instead of professional portfolios. Prospects cannot see proof. Ads create interest, but trust does not convert at scale.

**Discovery questions**
- Can a prospect see your best recent jobs in one professional link?
- Do field teams upload project photos somewhere the sales team can send instantly?

## 5. Product suite (what you can sell)

SegmiQ is **two complementary products**, shared login and database, billed separately. Most clients start with CRM; many add Cloud once they see portfolio selling.

### SegmiQ CRM — capture and close

The revenue operating system: lead capture, scoring, routing, WhatsApp automation, quotations, inbox, marketing, billing, and intelligence.

**Three portals (same company, different jobs):**

| Portal | Who | What they do |
|--------|-----|--------------|
| **Client Manager** | Owner / sales manager | Pipeline, Team Inbox, Marketing, Reports, Team, Billing |
| **Salesperson** | Field reps / agents | Hot leads, follow-ups, WhatsApp Sales Hub, quotes, call logging |
| **Agency / Super Admin** | SegmiQ or agency partners | Multi-client management, Meta setup, proposals, platform ops |

There is also a **Solo** mode for owner-operators without a full sales team.

### SegmiQ Cloud — document and prove

Project documentation and public portfolios so completed work wins the next job.

- Store project photos and media
- Share professional public links (no download required)
- Milestone / timeline storytelling of the job
- Public business portfolio (`{business}.segmiq.com`) that can capture new leads into CRM
- Watermarking so competitors cannot steal the work
- Field mobile / PWA for on-site upload on slow data

### Mobile apps

| App | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **SegmiQ Sales** (Android) | Today’s leads, follow-ups, offline-friendly sales work |
| **SegmiQ Cloud Field** (Android) | On-site photo capture and upload |

## 7. Industry talk tracks

Tailor the same product to the client’s world.

### Solar

- **Pain:** Load-shedding floods enquiries; first quote usually wins; techs are on roofs.
- **Pitch:** Capture Facebook/WhatsApp leads → score residential vs commercial / backup vs off-grid → route to nearest installer → one-tap send past installs and packages (5kW, 10kW) → Cloud documents the install for the next neighbour sale.
- **Close line:** “When the power is out, the fastest professional reply wins. SegmiQ makes that your default.”

### Construction

- **Pain:** Site enquiries are messy; quotes drag; proof of past builds is hard to share.
- **Pitch:** Centralise enquiries → track quote status → send portfolios and documents → Cloud timelines prove capability on complex jobs.
- **Close line:** “You don’t win construction on a spreadsheet — you win on speed, clarity, and visible past work.”

### Roofing

- **Pain:** Seasonal spikes; many quotes; hard to see which sizes/sources close; stale quotes die.
- **Pitch:** Score and prioritise → recover stale quotes → win analysis on quote size and source → Cloud before/after proof.
- **Close line:** “Stop guessing which roofs close. SegmiQ shows the pattern and recovers the quiet ones.”

### Electrical & landscaping

- **Pain:** Field teams; follow-ups slip; proof of finished work sells the next street.
- **Pitch:** Daily coaching + follow-up reminders → one-tap send of completed jobs → Cloud field upload.
- **Close line:** “Your crew’s finished work becomes tomorrow’s best sales asset.”

### Real estate

- **Pain:** Website and portal enquiries scatter; agents own chats; listings and leads disconnect.
- **Pitch:** Website API + listings context → route to the right agent → WhatsApp confirmation → pipeline and reports for the agency owner.
- **Close line:** “Every listing enquiry becomes a tracked opportunity — not a WhatsApp that vanished.”

## 10. Demo flow (recommended)

Aim for 20–30 minutes. Use their industry language.

1. **Open on pain (3 min)** — Where do leads come from? How fast do you reply? What happens when a rep leaves?
2. **Capture (4 min)** — Conversational form + instant WhatsApp confirmation + Facebook lead landing in pipeline.
3. **Score & route (4 min)** — Show score, priority, magic-link assignment to a salesperson.
4. **Sales Hub (5 min)** — Hot leads / Needs reply / Follow-up due — “this is the rep’s morning.”
5. **Convert (4 min)** — One-tap send + quotation on WhatsApp.
6. **Manager view (3 min)** — Response time, team activity, loss/location insights.
7. **Cloud teaser (3 min)** — Share link / portfolio — “this is how the next job is won.”
8. **Plan & next step (3 min)** — Recommend Starter / Growth / Scale; book onboarding; load a sample of their real leads if possible.

**Best practice:** Demo on a sample of *their* real leads whenever you can. That is a SegmiQ differentiator in the sales process itself.
